# Image references for "Making Clarity feel alive"

The research images for [feel-alive.md](feel-alive.md) are kept outside this
public repo. They are other apps' screenshots, carrying Appllama's watermark.

Each file below lists its panels from left to right as app, then
`app_id/screen_id`, then the screen name.

- **To see a panel again:** pass the ref to Appllama's `get_screen` as
  `screen_ref`. It costs one credit.
- **Prefixes:** refs starting `onb_` are onboarding screens and `spl_` are
  welcome screens. The rest are inside the product.
- **`*_sheet.jpg` files** are grids of frames from one video.
- **Screen names are Appllama's own labels, and a few are off:**
  - Daylio `oth_k1kne` is labelled "Edit Mood Entry" but shows the add menu.
  - Gratitude Plus has two different screens named "Home Feed".

Folders: `picks/` holds the chosen examples, `montages/` the working sets,
`video-frames/` the video grids, and `screens/` the single screens.
